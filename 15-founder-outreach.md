## Message templates (LinkedIn connection notes are limited to about 300 characters, so use the short version there)

**Short (connection request)**
> Hi [name], my name's Ewan Hetherington. I'm a Durham graduate building a fresh, real-food protein snack (egg, quark, oats) for athletes. I'd really value 15 minutes of your advice if you're ever open to it. Thanks!

**JNCK (Alex or Sean): after they accept**
> Hi [name], thanks for connecting. My name's Ewan Hetherington. I'm on the Durham Venture School programme, building Graft: a fresh-baked protein snack made from egg, quark and oats, starting with university sports squads. I read about JNCK and really respected how openly you shared what happened. If you'd be up for a 15-minute call, I'd love to ask:
> 1. What would you do differently if you started JNCK again?
> 2. What did you learn about selling a fresh, "healthier" baked product?
> 3. Anything you'd warn a first-time food founder about?
> No worries at all if you're busy. Cheers, Ewan

**Mooski (Robert Broome)**
> Hi Robert, my name's Ewan Hetherington. I'm a student founder in Durham, UK, building a chilled, real-food protein snack (egg, quark, oats) for athletes. Mooski is the closest thing I've found to what I'm trying to do. Could I ask you three quick questions, by message or a 15-minute call?
> 1. How did you handle shelf life and cold chain early on?
> 2. When did you move from making it yourself to a manufacturer?
> 3. What surprised you most about the refrigerated aisle?

**Perfect Bar / RXBAR (general)**

[Perfect Bar / RXBAR] is a big inspiration. If you ever had 15 minutes, I'd love one piece of advice: what mattered most in the first year? Thanks for reading, Ewan

## Tips
- Send 2–3 a week and log replies here.
- Before any call, read up on their story so your questions are specific.
- Ask at the end: "Is there anyone else you think I should talk to?"
- A reply from any of these founders is great material for the "Team" section of your pitch ("I've spoken to the founder of…").
