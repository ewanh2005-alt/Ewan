**Tips while you talk**
- **Keep it a conversation.** You don't have to ask every question. Follow what he says with "Why?", "Tell me more" and "Can you give me an example?".
- **If he's very active,** spend more time on sections 2 and 3. If not, note why the problem doesn't affect him; that's useful too.
- **Write down his exact words** when he says something strong; those are your pitch quotes. Then add a row to `research/17_interview_template.csv`.
