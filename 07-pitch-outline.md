| "Are your numbers real?" | Lab analysis result (budgeted Week 7) |
| "Is 'low sugar' legal?" | Yes: ≤5 g/100 g; ours is 3.0–3.8 g/100 g, confirmed by lab test before use (04 §5) |
| "How big is the market?" | Bottom-up: Durham active students × occasions × price, then BUCS. **Build this in Week 12 with pilot frequency data** `[GAP]` |
