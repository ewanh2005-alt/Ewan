| Likely question | Answer from |
|---|---|
| "Why not just drink chocolate milk and eat a banana?" | Honest: it works; we're for when there's no fridge or shop. Pilot data on who actually bought (02, 07) |
| "RXBAR failed here. Why won't you?" | Different job (after training, carbs + protein), different channel (clubs, not grocery). But admit it's a risk (02) |
| "17 g protein? Grenade and Warrior have 20." | True. We're "high protein" by law (25% of energy) but chose kitchen ingredients and ~2 g sugar over isolates, gelatine and polyols. |
| "Warrior RAW is £1. Why pay £2.75?" | No sweeteners, polyols or gelatine, and a laxative warning on theirs. We'll test whether athletes pay for that (price test £2.00–2.75). Pilot question tests whether buyers mind (07 §3) |
| "It needs a fridge. How will that scale?" | Fresh first (clubs, cafés, gyms); shelf-stable v2 with dried milk/egg for retail later (04 §4b) |
| "What stops Grenade or Veloforte copying you?" | Not much on recipe; our edge is community, speed and evidence. Be candid (02 Step 5) |
| "Are your numbers real?" | Lab analysis result (budgeted Week 7) |
| "Is 'low sugar' legal?" | Yes: ≤5 g/100 g; ours is 3.0–3.8 g/100 g, confirmed by lab test before use (04 §5) |
