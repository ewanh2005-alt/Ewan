# 04b — Make one T20 bar (test recipe) + Tesco shopping list

**Date:** 8 Oct 2026 · **Numbers from:** `04-nutrition.py` ingredient values (calculated, not lab-tested).
**Purpose:** a single test bar, so you can check taste and texture before making a full tin. Make 3–4 single bars over a few days and adjust.

## What one bar should give you (calculated)
| Protein | Carbs | Sugar | Fat | Fibre | Energy | Weight after baking |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **20.9 g** | **26.5 g** | **5.1 g** | **6.1 g** | **6.9 g** | **258 kcal** | ~100–115 g |

All of the batter becomes one bar, so the protein, carbs and other nutrients don't change with baking. Only the weight (and moisture) does, depending on how much water steams off.

## Ingredients for ONE bar
| Ingredient | Amount | Kitchen measure |
|---|---|---|
| Egg whites | **60 g** | 2 large egg whites (or 60 g carton egg white) |
| Whole egg, beaten | **15 g** | 1 tablespoon of a beaten egg (beat 1 egg in a cup, use 1 tbsp, keep the rest for breakfast) |
| Fat-free quark | **45 g** | 3 level tablespoons |
| Oat bran | **25 g** | ~3 heaped tablespoons |
| Rolled porridge oats | **15 g** | ~2 tablespoons |
| Cocoa powder (unsweetened) | **4 g** | 2 level teaspoons |
| Chia seeds | **2 g** | ½ teaspoon |
| Honey | **3 g** | ½ teaspoon |
| Salt | a tiny pinch | — |

**Use kitchen scales** (to 1 g). Spoon measures are only a guide; 5 g either way changes the numbers.

## Equipment
- Digital kitchen scales (1 g accuracy)
- A **small** ovenproof tin or dish, about 12 × 8 cm (a mini loaf tin or small silicone mould works), so the batter sits ~2.5 cm deep
- Baking paper, a bowl, a fork or small whisk, measuring spoons
- Optional but recommended: a probe thermometer (~£10)

## Step by step (≈ 45 minutes, most of it waiting)
1. **Heat the oven** to **160 °C fan / 180 °C conventional / gas 4**.
2. **Line the tin** with baking paper. **Weigh the empty lined tin** and write it down.
3. **Separate 2 eggs.** Put the whites in a bowl (≈60 g; top up from a third white if short). In a cup, beat 1 whole egg and add **1 tablespoon (15 g)** of it to the bowl.
4. **Add the quark and honey** to the bowl. Whisk with a fork for ~30 seconds until smooth and slightly frothy.
5. **Add the dry ingredients:** oat bran, oats, cocoa, chia and the pinch of salt. Stir until no dry patches remain. It'll be a thick, wet batter.
6. **Rest 10 minutes.** The oat bran and chia soak up liquid, so the bar holds together.
7. **Pour into the tin** and level the top. **Weigh the full tin** and write it down (batter weight = full − empty; it should be ~169 g).
8. **Bake 25–30 minutes** until the top is firm and springs back, the edges are pulling away, and a skewer in the centre comes out clean. If you have a probe, the **centre should reach 75 °C** (cooks the egg safely).
9. **Weigh straight out of the oven** and write it down. Steam lost = batter − baked. Send me the three weights.
10. **Cool** 10 minutes in the tin, lift out on the paper, cool fully on a rack, then **chill 1 hour**. It firms up as it cools.
11. **Taste test**, then score it: taste 1–9, texture 1–5 (1 = wet/eggy, 5 = firm, bar-like), and note anything ("too eggy", "dry", "needs more cocoa").
12. **Store** in the fridge, covered. Eat within **3 days**.

### If it's not right
| Problem | Fix next time |
|---|---|
| Too wet or custard-like | Bake 5–10 min longer, or swap 10 g quark for 10 g extra oat bran |
| Too eggy | Add ½ tsp more cocoa and a little cinnamon; reduce whites by 10 g and add 10 g quark |
| Too dry or crumbly | Bake 5 min less; add 1 tsp milk |
| Not sweet enough | Add ½ tsp more honey (adds ~2 g sugar; still under 6 g) |
| Falls apart | Rest the batter 15 min; add another ½ tsp chia |

## Safety and allergens
- **Allergens:** **egg, milk** (quark), **oats** (cereal containing gluten). No nuts in this recipe.
- Cook until set (centre 75 °C). Keep chilled. Don't give test bars to anyone with these allergies.
- Before handing bars to other people regularly (taste tests, pilots), **register as a food business** with your council (free; 28 days' notice).
