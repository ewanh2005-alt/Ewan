How it will work

Baked fresh, not stored for a year.

  1. Order weekly

    Individually, or as a squad through your coach.

  2. Baked to order

    Small batches, no long-life additives.

  3. Delivered chilled

    Around Durham to start, dropped at training.

  4. Fridge or freezer

    Eat within a few days, or freeze for later.

Questions

Good to know.

Can I buy it?

Not yet. Graft is a student venture in development. Join the waitlist to hear about free taste tests and trials first.

Why does it need the fridge?

Because it's real food with no preservatives, it's kept chilled like fresh food. You can freeze it to keep it longer.

What are the allergens?

Egg, milk and oats (gluten). Some flavours contain peanuts. Made in a kitchen that handles peanuts.

Why the name Graft?

Graft is British slang for hard work. It's for people who put the work in.
