# 14: Taste test kit (MMA gym first)

**Date:** 13 Oct 2026 · **Use when:** a bake scores at least 7/9 when you taste it yourself and when 2–3 friends try it (`11-plan-to-week-13.md`).
**Not legal advice.** For anything unclear, check with Durham County Council's food team; it's free.

---

## 1. Do you need a formal "agreement"?
**No contract is needed.** For a free taste test you need three things:
1. **The gym owner's permission.** A message or email they reply "yes" to is enough. Keep it.
2. **Each taster's informed consent.** They read what's in it (allergens) and agree before eating. A one-page sheet they tick or sign does this.
3. **Safe food:** basic hygiene, clear allergen info, and nobody tasting who has an allergy to the ingredients.

**Food business registration:**
- The FSA says you must register if you regularly make and supply food, **even if it's free**.
- A one-off tasting with people you know is low-risk. But you plan to keep testing, then sell. So **register now**. It's free, takes 28 days, and covers you for the gym test and the pilot.
- Do the Level 2 Food Hygiene course (about £15 online) before the gym test.

**Insurance:** not needed for a small free tasting `[ASSUMPTION]`. You will need public and product liability insurance before you sell (`10-scale-plan.md`).

## 5. Running it

**What to test**
- **Samples:** your best flavour vs **Barebells** vs one other bar (Grenade or Trek High Protein).
- **Sample size:** cut everything to the **same size and shape** (about 2 cm cubes). Take off any wrappers out of sight.
- **Codes:** label plates with random 3-digit codes (e.g. 417 / 238 / 951). Keep the key on your phone, not on the table.
- **Order:** rotate which sample each person tastes first (ABC, BCA, CAB…), so the first bite doesn't bias things. Water between samples.

**Kit**
- a cool bag with ice packs; keep your bar chilled until serving
- plates, cocktail sticks, napkins
- cups and water
- pens, the score cards and the info sheets
- hand gel, bin bags, a printed allergen sign

**Hygiene**
- Wash your hands, use clean utensils, and cut the samples at home.
- Bring the homemade bar out within about 2 hours of it leaving the fridge `[ASSUMPTION: standard chilled-food practice]`. Bin leftovers that have been out.

**Targets**
- **15–20 tasters** gives a useful early signal.
- Don't hover while they score, or they'll be polite.

**Pass marks** (from `13-pitch-friday-brief.md` and `07-mvp-pilot-plan.md`)
- Overall liking for your bar **≥7/9**.
- **≥50%** answer "probably" or "definitely" buy.
- Not last on texture.

**Afterwards**
- Type the scores into a spreadsheet. I can do the averages and charts if you send them.
- Photo the setup for the pitch. Get consent before photographing anyone's face.
- Thank the gym publicly, and invite tasters to the waitlist and pilot week.
