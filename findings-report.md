## A. Market & competitor findings

**A1. Styrkr BAR+ is isolate- and syrup-based, despite "all natural" marketing.** · High
- Ingredients start with glucose syrup, then soy protein crispies (isolated soy protein), caster sugar, invert syrup and soy protein isolate.
- Per 74 g bar: 15 g protein · 45–47 g carbs · 28 g sugar · **0.7 g fibre** · ~285 kcal · £2.75–2.85.
- *Evidence:* Sigma Sports, Bike24 and Halfords listings.
- *Use:* "Styrkr proved athletes pay ~£2.80 for recovery. We're building the one a dietitian would recommend."

**A2. Veloforte Forza is the closest product, and its weakness is protein.** · High
- Whole-food (apricot, almond, egg white): 12 g protein · 38 g carbs · 7.4 g fibre · 27.6 g sugar.
- Veloforte's 20 g bar (Protein Crunch) has only 16–18 g carbs and gets 5 g of its protein from collagen.
- *Use:* nobody, including Veloforte, does full protein + full carbs from whole food.

**A3. The 20 g-protein bars are the wrong design for refuelling.** · High
- Grenade, Barebells and PhD reach 20 g protein with 15–20 g polyols and <2 g sugar.
- Polyols are **excluded** from the legal carb-recovery claim.
- Grenade's pack warns of a laxative effect.

**A4. The real competition is chocolate milk, a banana, or nothing.** · Medium
- A 2019 meta-analysis found chocolate milk similar or better than placebo and other recovery drinks, though the evidence is limited.
- *Use:* the pitch must answer "why not chocolate milk?". Our answer: dosed to your weight, shelf-stable in a kit bag (no fridge), high fibre, whole-food protein.

**A5. Distribution is split by segment.** · Medium
- *Endurance:* Styrkr, Veloforte and TORQ sell through cycle retail (Sigma, Halfords, Upgrade distribution).
- *Team sport:* SiS (Man Utd, Celtic, Aston Villa, Saracens, 100+ pro football clubs), Myprotein (Sale Sharks) and Grenade (Scottish Rugby) own the **pro** clubs.
- **University and grassroots squads have no visible natural-bar supplier** (Low confidence; test in interviews).

**A6. Club reward schemes exist, so the club model alone isn't novel.** · High
- XMiles runs club discount and credit-back codes for running, cycling, football and other clubs.
- *Use:* our edge is a product built for **mixed-size squads**, not the discount.

**A7. Market-size numbers online conflict, so don't use them.** · High
- Estimates range from USD 1.1 bn to 2.7 bn.
- Credible anchor: Circana puts UK sports nutrition at **~£1.1 bn, +19% YoY**, and grocery protein bars + shakes at **£230 m, +7.5%** (via trade press).
- *Use:* build a bottom-up market size from club and university athlete numbers (Stage 3).

**A8. Company facts.** · Medium
- Styrkr: founded 2018 by Christian Sanderson; ~19 staff; new CEL50 carb chew Jul 2026; no public funding found.
- Veloforte: founded 2016, London, by Marc and Lara Giusti; no public funding found.

## B. Science findings

**B1. Protein dose per recovery feed ≈ 0.25–0.31 g/kg.** · High
- [Moore 2018, PMC6746967](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6746967/)
- 60 kg → 15–19 g · 75 kg → 19–23 g · 100 kg → 25–31 g.

**B2. Fibre is under-recommended for athletes.** · High (source) / Medium (it's a review, not a trial)
- Mancin, Burke & Rollo (2025, *Sports Medicine*, **open access** [PMC12106500](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12106500/)) suggest athletes eating <20 g/day **ramp to ~30 g/day including ~2 g beta-glucan over ~6 weeks**, for gut microbiome and gut-barrier benefits.

**B3. The type of fibre matters for gut comfort.** · Medium
- Chicory root, inulin and FOS are **high-FODMAP**, and FODMAPs are linked to exercise-related gut symptoms.
- Low-FODMAP and reduced-fibre strategies cut symptoms in runners.
- *Use:* get fibre from **oat bran, oats, chia and psyllium**, not chicory or inulin. Veloforte Crunch uses chicory root syrup.
- [Review PMC12845378](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12845378/) · [Monash FODMAP](https://monashfodmap.com/blog/low-fodmap-sports-fuelling-guide/)

**B4. Overnight ("24-hour") protein needs ~40 g, so one bar can't do it.** · High
- [Res 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22330017/)
- *Use:* drop "24-hour recovery" as a product claim.

**B5. Tart cherry recovery evidence comes from Newcastle (Northumbria University).** · High
- A 14-study meta-analysis found better strength recovery.
- **But** there is no authorised health claim, and a bar can't reach the studied doses.
- *Use:* flavour and local story only.

**B6. Fast carb refuel needs ~1.0–1.2 g/kg/h.** · Medium
- A bar can only **kick-start** this (45 g ≈ 0.6 g/kg for a 75 kg athlete).
- *Use:* be honest. The refuel QR tells athletes what to add.

## C. Product findings (new this update: higher fibre)

**C1. Whole-food protein tops out at ~10–11 g. Milk powder + egg white gets to ~20 g honestly.** · Medium (recipe maths)

**C2. A high-fibre version is achievable: ~11 g fibre, ~2 g oat beta-glucan, ~20 g protein, ~42 g carbs, ~98 g, ~345 kcal.** · Medium (to be lab-tested)

**Energy:** 20.5×4 + 41.6×4 + 8.2×9 + 10.8×2 (fibre) = 82 + 166 + 74 + 22 = **~344 kcal**
**Per segment (÷4):** ~5.1 g protein · ~10.4 g carbs · **~2.7 g fibre** · ~86 kcal

**C3. Fibre options compared** · Medium

| Option | Fibre/bar | Bar weight | Carbs | Gut risk | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard (Stage 2) | ~6 g | ~88 g | ~45 g | Low | Superseded |
| **High-fibre whole-food (C2)** | **~11 g** | **~98 g** | **~42 g** | Low–medium | **Recommended** |
| Max fibre with chicory/inulin | 14 g+ | ~98 g | ~40 g | **High (FODMAP)** | Rejected: gut risk, and not whole food |

**Pushback, briefly:** more fibre costs ~3 g of carbs and ~10 g of bar weight. It is fine *post*-exercise, but **never market it as a pre- or during-exercise bar.** The snap segments help: an athlete can eat 2 segments straight after and 2 later, which spreads the fibre load **without** us making a "24-hour" claim.

**C4. The high-fibre bar unlocks two extra legal claims.** · High (regulation) / Medium (our numbers)
- **"High fibre"** needs ≥6 g/100 g; ours is 10.8/98 = **11.0 g/100 g ✓**.
- **Oat beta-glucan cholesterol claim** needs ≥1 g per portion and must tell consumers the benefit comes from 3 g/day; ours is 2.1 g ✓ ([Reg. 1160/2011](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1160/data.html)). It is off-message for athletes, so use it only if it helps parents or older athletes.
- **"High protein"** (≥20% energy): 82/344 = **23.8% ✓**.

**C5. Pitch-ready fibre line** · Medium
> *"One bar gives ~11 g fibre: over a third of the 30 g a day sports scientists now suggest for athletes, and the full ~2 g of oat beta-glucan in that recommendation."* (Mancin et al. 2025)

## D. Regulatory & operational findings

**D1. The legal carb-recovery claim needs mandatory wording on pack.** · High
- The pack must state the benefit comes from carbs at 4 g/kg within 4–6 h after exercise. Polyols are excluded.
- [Reg. 2015/7](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2015/7/annexes)

**D2. Never claim** "speeds recovery", "reduces soreness", "repairs muscle", "anti-inflammatory" or "24-hour recovery". · Medium
- No authorised claims were found for these.

**D3. Register as a food business with the council at least 28 days before you start, including handing out pilot bars.** It is free. · High
- [gov.uk – register a food business](https://www.gov.uk/food-business-registration)
- **Action: register with Durham County Council or Newcastle City Council (wherever you'll make the bars) by ~Week 6, so a Week 9–10 pilot is legal.**

**D4. Informed Sport costs ~£400–800 per batch (third-party estimate) and needs a manufacturer.** · Low
- Phase 2, after DVS.

## What would change our minds (kill criteria)

| If we find… | Then… |
|---|---|
| <50% of interviewees would use a weight-based dose chart | Drop the snap bar hero; fall back to "full-dose, high-fibre whole-food bar" |
| >15% of pilot athletes report moderate gut symptoms | Cut fibre to ~6–8 g; remove figs/dates in favour of lower-FODMAP carbs |
| Athletes won't pay ≥£2.50 | Rework size and cost, or move to a club-subsidised model |
| A UK bodyweight-dosed bar launches | Lead with the high-fibre + whole-food protein combination and the club channel |
