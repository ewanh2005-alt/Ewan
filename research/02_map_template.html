Reading it honestly: the low-sugar corner is crowded, but every other bar in it is a lab protein bar built on protein blends or isolates, most with polyols (e.g. Grenade 16 g, PhD 20 g) or sweeteners. Every whole-food or "natural" bar we found sits at 9.5–38 g sugar. fettle is the only bar we found in the box made from kitchen ingredients alone. The trade-offs: it has less protein than the lab bars (~14 g vs ~20 g), and it's a fresh bake that needs a fridge. Not plotted (no sugar figure captured): SiS REGO, Huel, chocolate milk.
