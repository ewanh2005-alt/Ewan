For people who train MMA, BJJ, boxing, Muay Thai or wrestling

Two sessions done. Still hungry. Nothing in your bag but a sweetened lab bar.

We're making a snack for fighters to eat after training: real food, built around protein and carbs, with no sweeteners or sugar alcohols, and designed to last a day in a kit bag.

What we're building

  • Real-food proteinMade from egg white and oats. No sweeteners or sugar alcohols.
  • Carbs and protein togetherBuilt for the gap between your last session and your next meal.
  • Made for your kit bagFixed portions you can grab after training. We're testing how long it keeps out of the fridge before we promise anything.

Made by a fighter. I train MMA and run an MMA YouTube channel. After back-to-back sessions I wanted something better than a sweetened bar, so I'm making it with fighters and testing it at the gym.
