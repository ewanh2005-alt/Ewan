## Batch 13: Sponsorship and gyms (9 of 50)

- **UFC nutrition partners:** KNOX Hydration, co-founded by UFC champion Dricus du Plessis, is UFC's **hydration partner outside the US** (B13-001). **Thorne** remains the **performance-nutrition partner**, with supplements curated by UFC dietitians (B13-002). Elite fighters get dietitians and certified supplements; amateurs get neither.
- **Cage Warriors:** sponsors found are competitions, local brands and betting. **No nutrition partner found** (B13-003/004). That is an open slot later on.
- **Gyms:**
  - UK has **12.1m members and 7,463 clubs** (Leisure DB 2026), and UK Active's estimate is 12.2m, so the founder's figure is confirmed (B13-006).
  - **Counter-evidence:** one gym owner says **Mars and Snickers protein bars outsell specialist brands ~4:1**, because members buy familiar names (B13-005, anecdote). Gym counters may favour big brands, so H8/H11 need testing.
- **Certification:** Informed Sport example fees run **£3,750–£5,000** plus batch testing (B13-007). That is post-DVS.
- **Food-first:** UK dietitians (BDA 2022) endorse food-first because supplement contamination risk is higher. Cited research puts contamination at 12–58% (B13-008). **But a bar is still a manufactured product, so make no "clean for testing" claim** (B13-009).

## Batch 16: Manufacturing (6 of 50)

- **Wholebake** (9Bar, flapjacks, contract and own-label) opened a **third site in Wrexham, Oct 2025**, because its main site was fully utilised (B16-001). It's the obvious UK co-packer to approach, though it may not do baked egg-white bars.
- Trade terms benchmark: cases of 24, minimum 6 cases (B16-002).
- **Packaging:** recyclable mono-PP high-barrier flow-wrap exists (B16-003). Printed recyclable flowpacks are available **from 250 units** (B16-004), so pilot packaging is feasible.
- **SALSA:** ~**£775–£1,550 + VAT** via the Scotland programme (B16-005). Universities run SALSA support schemes (B16-006); ask DVS about a North East equivalent.
- **Gaps:** North East kitchen-hire rates (only US guides found, excluded); UK co-packer MOQs (only a directory claim of 5–50k units, excluded as unsourced).

## Batch 18: Pricing and students (5 of 50)

- **UK students spend ~£146 a month (~£34 a week) on groceries** (Save the Student 2026; B18-001). MoneySupermarket's estimate is £116 a month (B18-004).
- **67% skip meals at least sometimes** (2024; B18-002).
- **H5 implication:** one £2.50 bar a day (£17.50 a week) ≈ **half a student's weekly food budget**. Students and novices are probably **not** the early adopters at £2.50. Lead with working amateurs and fighters in camp; for students, offer packs (≈£2.00) or gym-subsidised boxes.
- **Van Westendorp caution:** survey price answers can be unstable (B18-005). **Validate with paid pre-orders.**
