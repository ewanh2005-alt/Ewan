Two sessions today and nothing real to eat in between?

A real-food snack built for MMA athletes who train twice a day. It lives in your kit bag, not the fridge.

  • Grab it between sessions: no cooking, no fridge, no shaker.
  • Made from food you recognise: real-food ingredients such as egg white and oats.
  • No artificial sweeteners or sugar alcohols: a short ingredient list you can read in five seconds.

How it's made: baked from real-food protein, with no artificial sweeteners or sugar alcohols. The recipe is in testing, so nutrition figures will be published only after independent lab analysis.

Who's making it
