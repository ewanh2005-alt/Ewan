## 3. Recipe cards (one 20 × 20 cm tin → 9 bars of ~90 g)

Weigh everything. 1 large egg ≈ 58 g out of the shell; 1 white ≈ 33 g.

### A. Cocoa & Peanut Butter (hero, no honey)
| Ingredient | Amount | Why it's in |
|---|---|---|
| Large free-range eggs | 6 (≈348 g) | Protein; binds and sets the bar |
| Extra egg whites | 5 (≈165 g), or a carton of liquid egg white | Lean protein |
| Rolled porridge oats | 240 g | Carbs, texture |
| Fat-free quark (or plain skyr) | 200 g | Lean protein (≈12%); moist texture; binds |
| Oat bran | 100 g | More protein (17%) and fibre than oats |
| Semi-skimmed milk | 50 ml | Mixing, flavour |
| Smooth peanut butter (100% peanuts) | 40 g | Flavour, protein |
| Pumpkin seeds | 30 g | Protein (30%), crunch |
| Cocoa powder (unsweetened) | 25 g | Flavour |
| Chia seeds | 20 g | Binds (gel), fibre, protein |
| Salt | a good pinch (1.5 g) | Flavour |

### B. Honey & Chia (nut-free recipe)
6 eggs · 5 extra whites · 250 g oats · 200 g quark · 100 g oat bran · 50 ml milk · 40 g pumpkin seeds · 25 g chia · 20 g honey (≈1 tbsp) · pinch of salt.

### C. Cocoa & Honey (nut-free recipe)
6 eggs · 5 extra whites · 240 g oats · 200 g quark · 100 g oat bran · 50 ml milk · 30 g pumpkin seeds · 25 g cocoa · 20 g chia · 20 g honey · pinch of salt.

### Method
1. Mix the chia with the milk and leave **10 minutes** to gel.
2. Oven **160 °C fan / 180 °C**. Line the tin; **weigh the empty tin**.
3. Whisk the eggs, whites, quark, peanut butter or honey, and the chia gel until smooth.
4. Stir in the oats, oat bran, cocoa, seeds and salt. Rest **10 minutes** so the oats and bran soak.
5. Spread into the tin, level it, **weigh the full tin**.
6. Bake **40–45 minutes** until firm, golden at the edges, and a skewer comes out clean (centre **75 °C** if you have a probe).
7. **Weigh straight out of the oven.** The model assumes **30%** of batter weight is lost as steam. Put your real figure into `loss=` in `04-nutrition.py` and re-run.
8. Cool, chill 1 hour, cut into **9** (or 18 halves), wrap, label, refrigerate.

**Spare yolks:** 5 whites leave 5 yolks per tin. Use them, or buy cartoned egg white (cheaper per bar).
**Optional higher-protein swap to test:** replace 40 g oats with **40 g gram (chickpea) flour** for ≈ +0.7 g protein per bar. It can taste beany; try it in round 2.

## 4. Tensions, honestly
- **Protein vs carbs:** the extra protein foods push protein to 25% of energy but bring carbs down to 23–25 g. The professor's ~10 g/100 kcal can't be met with whole foods *and* high carbs, because oats dilute protein (see `01b` table). We're at 6.2, the highest we found for a real-food bar.
- **Taste and texture (biggest risk):** more egg and quark makes it softer and more cake-like (estimated moisture ~33–35%). Blind-test **v6 vs v5 vs Grenade** on taste and texture (C3, C8, C9, E1 all stressed taste).
- **Fresh vs shelf-stable:** needs a fridge and a short use-by. Pilot through squad fridges and gym cafés. The shelf-stable route (dried milk and egg) stays the scale-up option.
- **Fibre:** 5–6 g; good for daily intake; not for before training.
- **Ingredient count:** 10–11 ingredients, more than v5's 7. All are still single foods you'd find in a supermarket.

- Use-by: start at **3 days**; confirm with your EHO. "Suitable for home freezing."
