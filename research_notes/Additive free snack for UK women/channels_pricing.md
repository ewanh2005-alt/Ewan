# UK Routes to Market, Channels and Pricing Economics for an Early-Stage Women-Focused Natural Protein/Fibre Snack Bar (as of 23 Oct 2026)

Note on method: web search plus fetches. Several key trade sites (The Grocer, Grocery Gazette, Just Food, May Insight) could not be fetched directly in this session (DNS errors), so some findings rest on search-result snippets of those articles. These are flagged "(snippet)". Many cost and margin figures come from consultancy, agency or vendor blogs with commercial interests. They are labelled as such and should be treated as planning ranges, not authoritative figures.

## Q1. Where does each segment buy snack and protein bars, and is there channel-share data?

### Takeaway
The only quantified channel data is from paid market-research summaries. They put supermarkets and hypermarkets at about 49-54% of UK snack and protein bar sales (2025), and I found no Holland & Barrett-specific share figure. No source gave segment-level (18-26 vs 35-55+) channel data, so the segment split below is inference.

### Cited Findings
- Supermarkets and hypermarkets held 54.10% of the UK snack bar market in 2025 — [Mordor Intelligence, UK Snack Bar Market](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/united-kingdom-snack-bar-market) (paid report summary)
- Supermarkets held 54.3% of UK snack bars in 2025 — [MarketDataForecast](https://www.marketdataforecast.com/market-reports/united-kingdom-snack-bars-market) (paid report summary)
- Supermarkets and hypermarkets held about 49% of UK protein bar sales — [The Report Cubes, UK Protein Bar Market](https://www.thereportcubes.com/report-store/protein-bar-market-uk). Grand View Research gives 38.4% for the *global* protein bar market in 2025 — [Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/protein-bar-market-report). The definitions differ (snack vs protein bars, UK vs global).
- Health food shops are described only qualitatively, as stocking premium clean-label and organic lines, with no share figure — [MarkWide Research](https://markwideresearch.com/united-kingdom-snack-bar-market)
- TikTok Shop is now a grocery and protein channel. In 2025 Lidl GB became the first UK supermarket to sell on TikTok Shop, launching with protein bundles at £5 — [Lidl GB press release, 2025](https://corporate.lidl.co.uk/media-centre/pressreleases/2025/tiktok-shop-protein). A later report says the protein products sold out within 18 minutes (search summary, secondary).
- TikTok Shop is growing in UK grocery, including fresh and baked goods — [Grocery Gazette interview, 28 Mar 2025](https://www.grocerygazette.co.uk/2025/03/28/interview-tiktok-shop-fresh/) (snippet)
- Holland & Barrett actively lists women's-health snack bars. Fem's period-nutrition bar range (3 SKUs) secured 450 H&B stores from 3 August (2026, per search dating) — [The Grocer](https://www.thegrocer.co.uk/news/period-nutrition-bar-secures-holland-and-barrett-listing/721686.article) (snippet). The bars are on H&B online — [H&B product page](https://www.hollandandbarrett.com/shop/product/fem-bar-salted-caramel-40-g-6100028861)
- H&B is piloting an "ingestible beauty" focus and reported a 7% sales uplift (Sept 2025) — [CosmeticsDesign Europe](https://www.cosmeticsdesign-europe.com/Article/2025/09/11/holland-barrett-pilots-ingestible-beauty-focus-with-7-sales-uplift/). This shows the retailer is leaning into female-targeted wellness.

### Inferences
- Segment A (18-26, gym-going) is most reachable through TikTok and TikTok Shop, gym and boutique-studio fridges and counters, university campus outlets (SU shops, campus convenience), and grab-and-go convenience. Large brands such as Grenade and Fulfil dominate supermarket protein fixtures at about £2.90 a bar (see Q4).
- Segment B (35-55+) is more likely to buy in Holland & Barrett, Waitrose, Ocado, Boots, independents and farm shops, and DTC subscription. The Fem listing suggests H&B buyers are receptive to women's-health-positioned bars.
- Supermarkets carry about half of category volume, but they are a later-stage channel for a start-up because of the economics in Q3.

### Gaps
- No Kantar or NielsenIQ public data on protein-bar channel share by retailer, and no H&B, Boots, Ocado or Amazon share figures.
- No age- or gender-segmented channel data for bar purchasing found. This would need Kantar or Mintel (paid).
- No figures on gym or university channel size.

## Q2. How have recent UK challenger snack brands launched and grown? Case studies

### Takeaway
Concrete, independently verified 2025-26 UK case studies are scarce. The most relevant examples are Fem (women's-health bar into 450 H&B stores, 2026), Native Snacks (Ocado drove a 1,500% distribution increase, then Sainsbury's and H&B), Battle Oats (gyms and independents, then 300 H&B stores), and The Gut Stuff (Tesco incubator, high-fibre bars). TikTok Shop results for Proper Snacks and Grenade are agency self-reported.

### Cited Findings
- **Fem (women's period-nutrition bar):** A 3-SKU date-and-seed bar with added magnesium, iron, B6, B12, D, folate, omega-3 and creatine, priced at £2.25 per 40g, listed in 450 H&B stores. The brand claims it is the first national bricks-and-mortar listing for a menstrual-health snack bar — [The Grocer](https://www.thegrocer.co.uk/news/period-nutrition-bar-secures-holland-and-barrett-listing/721686.article); [The Grocer opinion, "Tampons to trail bars: women's health is getting creative"](https://www.thegrocer.co.uk/comment-and-opinion/tampons-to-trail-bars-womens-health-is-getting-creative/721875.article) (snippets). This is the closest direct competitor and comparable.
- **Native Snacks (popped lotus seeds):** An Ocado launch drove a 1,500% increase in distribution, followed by Sainsbury's and Holland & Barrett. The article is about 2 years old (c. 2024) — [The Grocer](https://www.thegrocer.co.uk/news/native-snacks-rebrands-ahead-of-sainsburys-and-holland-and-barrett-launch/652283.article) (snippet)
- **Battle Oats (Hull, protein flapjacks):** Built through independent health stores and gyms, then won supply to 300 H&B stores (older coverage, 2010s) — [Eat Drink Yorkshire](https://eatdrinkyorkshire.co.uk/hull-based-nutrition-brand-supply-300-holland-barrett-stores/). This is a northern regional analogue for the gym-to-H&B route.
- **Real Handful:** Its first major listings were Ocado and Holland & Barrett — [The Grocer](https://www.thegrocer.co.uk/news/real-handful-wins-listings-at-ocado-and-holland-and-barrett/532824.article) (snippet, older)
- **The Gut Stuff (high-fibre snack bars):** An alumnus of the Tesco Incubator/Accelerator — [Growsights](https://growsights.co.uk/blog/tesco-accelerator-2026-what-the-9-brands-mean-for-suppliers) (secondary)
- **Proper Snacks on TikTok Shop:** Sold out its first run within a month, with 600+ orders, 500K+ views in two weeks and a 4.9/5 rating. The agency handled listing optimisation, pricing, creator recruitment and content — [BeaSocial case study](https://www.beasocial.co.uk/work/propersnacks) (agency self-reported)
- **Grenade on TikTok Shop:** TikTok Shop revenue grew 414% in the first six months, including about 8 TikTok Lives a month — [Fluencers Group case study](https://www.fluencersgroup.com/blog-posts/case-study-grenades-tiktok-shop-success) (agency self-reported; an established brand, not a launch)
- **MyProtein x Vimto (TikTok Shop UK):** About $57K GMV in 30 days, attributed to a recognisable flavour hook — [Z Media](https://www.z.media/insights/how-to-launch-a-protein-brand-on-tiktok-shop-uk) (agency)
- **The Snax Lab:** A TikTok for Business case study on Shop Ads — [TikTok Ads](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/inspiration/the-snax-lab-succeeds-with-shop-ads)
- **Built Bar (US):** TikTok Shop GMV of about $20.4M, roughly 3.5x its category average — [FastMoss](https://www.fastmoss.com/blog/the-tiktok-shop-protein-bar-playbook-why-built-bar-is-winning/) (US; shows the category works on TikTok Shop)
- The Grocer has published healthier-snacking brand profiles — [The Grocer](https://www.thegrocer.co.uk/analysis-and-features/healthier-snacking-brand-profiles-from-snack-bars-to-sorghum-treats/667545.article) (not fetched)

### Inferences
- A credible sequence for this brand: DTC, TikTok Shop and local gyms/studios/campus (proof of demand, reviews, content) → independents via wholesalers or Faire, plus Ocado → H&B (women's-health positioning, as Fem shows) → Waitrose or Sainsbury's via an accelerator.
- The fact that Fem got into H&B at £2.25 per 40g shows the "female-health bar" shelf space is being claimed in 2026. Differentiation (gut-gentle, protein plus fibre, additive-free) has to be sharp.

### Gaps
- No verified data on university ambassador programmes for UK snack brands.
- No independent (non-agency) metrics on gym-sampling conversion.

## Q3. Retailer and distributor margins, listing fees, promotions, wholesalers, Faire, and supermarket start-up programmes (2025-26)

### Takeaway
For UK branded grocery, plan on retailer front margins of 30-45% of the ex-VAT shelf price (independents expect at least 35%) and wholesaler margins of about 15-30%, with margins compounding. A brand selling via a wholesaler to an independent may keep under 50% of the ex-VAT RRP. Faire charges 25% on first orders and 15% on reorders. Supermarket programmes active in 2026 include the Tesco Accelerator (6th cohort, Oct 2026), Sainsbury's Future Brands (relaunched Sept 2026, with a "Feel Good convenience" track) and an H&B x Breakout Wellness challenger-brand partnership (Sept 2026).

### Cited Findings
**Margins and fees**
- UK branded grocery front margin is about 30-45% of the shelf price ex-VAT, with health, beauty and premium lines at the higher end. Margins compound: a 35% retailer margin plus a 25% wholesaler margin leaves the brand 48.75% of the ex-VAT shelf price, not 40% — [GetGross, "How retailer margin actually works (UK grocery)"](https://www.getgross.co.uk/guides/retailer-margin) (consultancy blog)
- Independents expect at least 35% retail margin — [Woodford premium food wholesale guide](https://woodford.food/blog/premium-food-wholesale-a-guide-for-uk-retailers-and-brands) (wholesaler blog)
- Other ranges: wholesaler 10-25%, retailer 20-50%+ — [Herbs n Beans wholesale blog](https://wholesale.herbsnbeans.co.uk/blogs/news/the-difference-between-retail-and-wholesale-grocery-prices-explained)
- Retailer margin is 30-45%, distributor margin 20-30%, and trade spend 15-25% of wholesale revenue — [EightX](https://eightx.co/blog/food-brand-pricing-strategy) (US-oriented)
- A minimum £2,000 listing fee per wholesaler is reported as typical, before marketing costs — [Branding Innovations UK](https://brandinginnovations.co.uk/the-cost-of-marketing-your-food-or-drink-brand/) (agency blog, single source)
- Listing fees, promotional allowances, gondola ends, volume rebates and now retail media are normal costs of supermarket distribution — [KamCity](https://www.kamcity.com/kamlibrary/industry-issues/the-70-margin-business-inside-every-supermarket/)
- Distributors are said to cost food brands about 30% of margin, with promo deductions around 2-3% and slotting 1-4% — [Stretch Innovation](https://www.stretchinnovation.be/insights/why-distributors-cost-food-brands-30-margin) (Benelux)
- VAT: retailer margin is calculated on the ex-VAT price. Many snacks and confectionery are standard-rated at 20%. Cereal bars are often zero-rated, but chocolate-coated bars are standard-rated — [GetGross](https://www.getgross.co.uk/guides/retailer-margin); [Small Business Collaborative](https://www.smallbusinesscollaborative.co.uk/post/do-i-need-to-factor-vat-into-my-wholesale-pricing-if-im-not-vat-registered). The VAT status of a specific bar must be checked against HMRC guidance (not verified here).

**Wholesalers for independents**
- Suma is the UK's largest worker-owned co-op wholesaler, with over 45 years of trading and its own UK fleet (ambient, chilled, frozen) — [Suma](https://www.suma.coop/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suma_(co-operative))
- Infinity Foods (Brighton co-op) has a "Snack Bars & Flapjacks" category and supplies independent health food, farm shop and zero-waste stores — [Infinity Foods Wholesale](https://www.infinityfoodswholesale.coop/food-cupboard/convenience-food)
- CLF Distribution (Nursling) is listed in a UK wholefood wholesaler directory with Suma and Infinity — [Thursday Cottage wholesalers page](https://www.thursday-cottage.com/pages/wholesalers)
- There is an overview of UK food distributors for 2026 — [GourmetPro](https://www.gourmetpro.co/blog/uk-top-food-beverage-distributors/)

**Faire**
- 25% commission on a first order from a Faire-sourced retailer, 15% on reorders, and 0% on Faire Direct (retailers the brand invites), plus a flat transaction fee (about $10 per order cited) — [Craftybase](https://api.craftybase.com/blog/how-much-does-faire-charge). The 25% and 15% split is long-standing — [BevNET](https://www.bevnet.com/?p=181636)
- Brands outside North America may pay an additional 10% referral fee on first orders (single source, unverified) — [Craftybase](https://api.craftybase.com/blog/how-much-does-faire-charge)

**Supermarket and retailer start-up programmes (2025-26)**
- **Tesco Accelerator:** The sixth cohort of 9 brands was announced in early October 2026, each getting a year of mentoring from Tesco buyers. The 2026 cohort is mostly non-snack (olive oil, dumplings, Eaten Alive fermented food, coconut water and so on) — [The Grocer](https://www.thegrocer.co.uk/news/tesco-reveals-brand-winners-of-its-2026-accelerator-programme/724357.article); [Retail Bulletin, 5 Oct 2026](https://www.theretailbulletin.com/grocer/tesco-accelerator-programme-2026-nine-small-brands-selected-05-10-2026/)
- **Sainsbury's Future Brands:** Relaunched for 2026 (17 Sept 2026) with two category-specific recruitment campaigns, Mindful Drinking and "Feel Good" convenience. The latter seeks healthy, convenient ready-to-eat options for busy schedules — [Grocery Gazette, 17 Sep 2026](https://www.grocerygazette.co.uk/2026/09/17/sainsburys-launches-future-brands-programme/) (snippet). This conflicts with a blog saying Future Brands was folded into a Product Direction team in May 2025 and lacks an external "front door" — [Growsights](https://growsights.co.uk/blog/tesco-accelerator-vs-sainsburys-thrive-brand-listing-guide). The Sept 2026 Grocery Gazette report is more recent and authoritative.
- Sainsbury's innovation team on chasing "entrepreneurial heat" — [Marketing Week](https://www.marketingweek.com/sainsburys-future-brands-innovation/)
- **Holland & Barrett x Breakout Wellness UK (Sept 2026):** H&B will give 10 challenger brands advice and product-development support — [The Grocer H&B coverage](https://www.thegrocer.co.uk/holland-and-barrett/2706.subject) (snippet)
- An overview of five retailer incubator schemes (2025) — [May Insight](https://www.mayinsight.co.uk/post/retailer-incubator-schemes-2025) (not fetched). On why incubators work or don't — [The Grocer](https://www.thegrocer.co.uk/reports-and-data/brands-incubator-schemes-and-why-they-work-or-not/679188.article)

### Inferences
- The Sainsbury's "Feel Good convenience" track is the best-fit supermarket programme for an additive-free gut-friendly bar right now. Application windows should be checked immediately.
- Faire is attractive for gift and lifestyle independents (pastel branding suits boutiques and studios), but 25% first-order commission on top of about 35-50% retailer margin is heavy. Using Faire Direct links for retailers the founder recruits herself (0%) mitigates this.

### Gaps
- No published Suma, CLF or Infinity trade terms (margin, MOQ, listing or marketing fees). These need direct buyer contact.
- No published Waitrose, Ocado, Boots or H&B margin or listing-fee schedules.
- No primary confirmation of the Faire UK referral fee.
- The Sainsbury's 2026 application deadline was not found.

## Q4. Price benchmarks and how COGS, MOQs and margins stack up

### Takeaway
Mainstream supermarket protein bars sell for about £2.90-2.95 a bar in 2026 (Grenade £2.90 at Sainsbury's, Feb 2026; Grenade Soft Core RRP £2.95 per 50g, Aug 2026; Fulfil £2.90 single, about £2.40 in a multipack). A women's-health natural bar (Fem) sells at £2.25 per 40g in H&B. Co-manufacturer MOQs range from about 1,000-3,000 bars per SKU (stock base with custom wrapper) to 5,000-30,000 for bespoke recipes.

### Cited Findings
- Grenade Soft Core range RRP is £2.95 per 50g bar, launching August 2026 across supermarkets — [Grocery Gazette, 20 Jul 2026](https://www.grocerygazette.co.uk/2026/07/20/grenade-launches-soft-core-protein-bar-range/) (snippet)
- Grenade bars at Sainsbury's rose from £2.70 (Feb 2025) to £2.90 (Feb 2026), up 7.4% — [Trolley.co.uk price index](https://www.trolley.co.uk/grocery-price-index/brand_category_store/?brand=Grenade&category=Protein+Bars&store_id=3)
- Fulfil sells for £2.90 single at Tesco, and the 10 x 55g mixed pack for £24.00 (about £2.40 a bar) — [Olive Magazine](https://www.olivemagazine.com/reviews/best-protein-bars/); [Tesco](https://www.tesco.com/shop/en-GB/products/308485599)
- Fem bar sells for £2.25 per 40g at Holland & Barrett — [H&B](https://www.hollandandbarrett.com/shop/product/fem-bar-chocolate-40-g-6100028859)
- One market report estimates national protein brands at £1.50-2.50 a bar, which conflicts with the retailer listings above — [IndexBox](https://www.indexbox.io/store/united-kingdom-kw-protein-bars-snack-pack-840-market-analysis-forecast-size-trends-and-insights/) (estimate). Retailer listings are more reliable.
- **Co-manufacturer MOQs:** Nutrikal (EU) quotes 1,000-3,000 bars per SKU for a stock base formulation with custom wrapper, and about 5,000+ a batch for bespoke recipes — [Suppliers.ai](https://suppliers.ai/blog/best-private-label-protein-bar-manufacturers). Aggregators put custom protein bar MOQs at 10,000-30,000 units — [Wonnda](https://wonnda.com/product-types/protein-bar-manufacturers/). UK co-man Opalbond says MOQs vary by factory and spec — [Opalbond](https://opalbond.co.uk/our-work/protein-bars). UK co-man Food & Nutrition Partners makes bars and bites on extrusion lines — [FNP](https://foodnutritionpartners.co.uk/what-we-do/bars-bites/)
- Per-bar co-man cost: the only explicit figure is US, at about $0.30-$2.00 a bar depending on ingredients, size and volume — [Keychain](https://www.keychain.com/manufacturer/protein-bar) (US, rough)
- **Price waterfall example:** at a £1.50 RRP with 20% VAT, the ex-VAT price is £1.25. After a 35% retailer margin the brand gets £0.81 direct, or about £0.61 via a 25% wholesaler — [GetGross](https://www.getgross.co.uk/guides/retailer-margin)

### Inferences (illustrative calculation, not sourced figures)
- Assume an RRP of £2.50 (between Fem at £2.25 and Grenade/Fulfil at £2.90-2.95) and a standard-rated bar (20% VAT). Ex-VAT is £2.08. With a 40% retailer margin the brand gets about £1.25 direct. Via a 25% wholesaler the brand gets about £0.94. Via a first Faire order at 25%, a £1.25 wholesale price nets about £0.94 before fees. If the bar is zero-rated, the ex-VAT price is the full £2.50 and the economics improve markedly, so VAT classification matters.
- For a brand to survive about 15-25% trade spend and still reach about 40-50% gross margin, delivered COGS (bar, wrapper, case, freight) probably needs to be at or below about £0.50-0.70 a bar at a £2.50 RRP. Clean-label protein and fibre ingredients (nuts, dates, whey or pea isolate, chicory or other fibres) are costlier than mainstream bars, so small-batch COGS may be above this at first. DTC (keeping the full ex-VAT price minus fulfilment and CAC) is the margin-rich launch channel.
- A first run of about 3,000-5,000 bars per SKU across 2-3 SKUs implies roughly 6,000-15,000 bars of initial inventory.

### Gaps
- No UK-sourced per-bar co-man cost for a clean-label protein and fibre bar. Itemised quotes are needed (MOQ price, 2x MOQ price, development, artwork and film costs).
- 2026 shelf prices for Nakd, Deliciously Ella, Tribe, Misfits, Bounce, Graze and others were not confirmed.
- Whether a protein/fibre bar without chocolate coating is zero-rated was not confirmed with HMRC.

## Q5. Customer acquisition: TikTok/Instagram for young women vs Facebook and other channels for older women, and micro-influencer economics

### Takeaway
UK micro-influencer (10K-50K) rates in 2026 are about £250-1,000 per Instagram post and £300-1,200 per TikTok video, with nano creators much cheaper. A cost per engagement of £0.05-0.20 is called reasonable. No UK food-category CAC data, and no age-split (TikTok vs Facebook) acquisition-cost data, was found.

### Cited Findings
- UK micro (10K-50K) rates are £250-1,000 per Instagram post, £300-1,200 per TikTok video and £500-2,000 per YouTube integration — [Gigapay UK Influencer Rates 2026](https://www.gigapay.com/blog/uk-influencer-rates-2026)
- Micro (10K-100K) is £80-1,500 per post, with nano the cheapest entry point — [Whito, Influencer & UGC Rates UK 2026](https://whito.co.uk/research/influencer-ugc-rates-uk/)
- Reels and TikToks cost 25-50% more than static posts — [Aether Agency rate card 2026](https://aether-agency.co.uk/insights/influencer-rate-card-uk-2026)
- Sources conflict on whether TikTok is cheaper than Instagram at nano and micro tiers, or equal or higher in the UK — [Influee](https://influee.co/gb/blog/tiktok-influencer-rates); [Gigapay](https://www.gigapay.com/blog/uk-influencer-rates-2026)
- A claim that micro rates have risen 200-233% since 2024 is unverified — [Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/influencer-rates/micro-influencer-rates/)
- A cost per engagement of £0.05-0.20 is reasonable in the UK (2026). At micro tier on TikTok, about $0.15-0.40 is typical and over $0.50 is high — [Whito](https://whito.co.uk/research/influencer-ugc-rates-uk/); [Influee](https://influee.co/gb/blog/tiktok-influencer-rates)
- TikTok Shop launch playbooks rely on creator affiliates plus Lives (Grenade ran about 8 Lives a month) — [Fluencers Group](https://www.fluencersgroup.com/blog-posts/case-study-grenades-tiktok-shop-success); [Z Media](https://www.z.media/insights/how-to-launch-a-protein-brand-on-tiktok-shop-uk)

### Inferences
- For Segment A, TikTok Shop's affiliate model (commission per sale rather than a flat fee) plus product gifting to gym, Pilates and "girl's gym" nano and micro creators is the lowest-risk CAC route. Durham and North East university fitness societies are a natural seeding base.
- For Segment B, Facebook and Instagram paid social, Ocado and H&B in-store presence, women's-health podcasts and newsletters, menopause and gut-health communities, and sampling at farm shops and Fenwick-type department stores are more plausible. No sourced CAC benchmarks were found to compare.

### Gaps
- No sourced UK CAC figures for snack or food DTC, by platform or by age.
- No data on gifting-only conversion, or on Meta vs TikTok CPMs for UK women by age, in 2026.
