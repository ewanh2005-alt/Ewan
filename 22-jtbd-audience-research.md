## 2. Top 10 pains (with their words)
1. **Too sweet, chemical aftertaste.**
   - "All the others I have tried are way too sweet and/or have a horrid chemical aftertaste" [V], [Mumsnet](https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/the_weights_room/4863913-protein-bars).
   - "Quite sweet … Slightly bitter aftertaste" [V], [Men's Fitness on Barebells](https://mensfitness.co.uk/review/barebells-bar-review/).
   - "many comments lamented the sugar alcohols and artificial sweeteners that leave a bad aftertaste" [V], [Tasting Table](https://www.tastingtable.com/1929398/worst-flavor-barebells-protein-bar).
2. **Gut problems from sugar alcohols.**
   - "it's really severe with certain sugar alcohols. Maltitol, I'm looking at you!" [V], [MyFitnessPal](https://community.myfitnesspal.com/en/discussion/1191171/gas-stomach-cramps-from-protein-bars).
   - Bars contain "artificial sweeteners and sugar alcohols that don't agree with a lot of people" [V], Bodybuilding.com.
   - Grenade's label itself warns "excessive consumption may cause laxative effect" (17 g polyols per bar) [V], [Ocado](https://www.ocado.com/products/grenade-peanut-butter-jelly-protein-bar-581951011).
3. **"Glorified chocolate bar" / too processed.**
   - "the shop bought bars just have too many ingredients" [V], Mumsnet.
   - "full of other stuff making them no better than a chocolate bar" [V], Mumsnet.
   - "most 'protein bars' are candy bars with some extra protein" [P], [MyFitnessPal](https://community.myfitnesspal.com/en/discussion/652481/protein-shake-vs-protein-bars).
4. **Price.**
   - "three quid for a chocolate bar" [V], [UK-Muscle](https://www.uk-muscle.co.uk/threads/protein-bars.371395/).
   - "Protein bars = VERY expensive way of getting protein. A can of tuna (60p from asda) has just as much protein." [V], [UK-Muscle](https://www.uk-muscle.co.uk/threads/are-protein-bars-worth-it.162701/).
   - "just wish they were a bit more affordable" [V], Holland & Barrett (Grenade).
5. **No time to eat properly after training.**
   - "by the time you have showered and travelled home and prepared and eaten a meal you will be very pushed for time" [V], The Student Room.
   - 80% of athletes say their schedule limits cooking (S29).
6. **"Natural" options are dry and bland.**
   - "these bars are really just very very bland. They are dryer than a normal flapjack" [V], [Ocado, Trek](https://www.ocado.com/products/trek-original-oat-protein-flapjacks-multipack-304558011).
   - "very dry with hardly any flavour" [V], [H&B Ireland, Trek](https://www.hollandandbarrett.ie/shop/product/60016198).
7. **Cheap bars taste awful.** Aldi bars "taste like rotten cardboard" [V], The Student Room.
8. **Clean-label bars are low in protein.** Trek 9–10 g and Bio&Me 9 g per bar (labels, `02d` WR40, WR49). This comes from the labels, not complaints.
9. **Real food isn't portable.**
   - Doubts about yoghurt unchilled; hummus "goes pretty funky pretty quickly" [P/V], [Mumsnet](https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/food_and_recipes/4343461-No-fridge-high-protein-vegetarian-lunch-ideas).
   - Boiled eggs are peeled out of sight "because it's a bit smelly" [V], Mumsnet.
10. **Hungry late at night after hard training.** "if you are training hard you need to eat something reasonably substantial at night otherwise you will wake up hungry" [V], [Sherdog](https://forums.sherdog.com/threads/eating-late-after-bjj-mma-need-advice.810858/).

## 3. Try → pay → abandon
- **Try first:** whey shakes and supermarket bars (Grenade, Barebells, Fulfil, Warrior). Shakes: "the convenience factor of protein shakes is half of the attraction" [V], The Student Room.
- **What they'll pay:**
  - About **£1–1.50 is accepted**: "ASDA do sell some for a quid each" [V], The Student Room; Fulfil "for about 1.50 each" at B&M [V], Mumsnet.
  - **About £3 is resented** (UK-Muscle).
  - Full price is £2.90 per bar at Tesco/Ocado; people buy on Clubcard or offer (~£1.90–2) ([Trolley](https://www.trolley.co.uk/product/barebells-peanut-butter-protein-bar/QDH628)).
- **Why they abandon:**
  - sweetness or aftertaste
  - gut issues
  - reformulation (Quest: "a terrible aftertaste" [V], MyFitnessPal)
  - price
  - feeling it's a "chocolate bar"
- **Where they switch to:**
  - dairy: "I use to have protein bars… Now I have yogurts from Aldi, 25gm protein for 75p." [V], UK-Muscle
  - kvarg/skyr/quark (filling, "20g+" [P])
  - eggs, tuna, homemade bars
- **What makes them stay:** taste. They stick with the one bar they tolerate (Barebells, Fulfil).

## 4. Objections and fears (for a fresh real-food snack)
- **Fridge:** they distrust unchilled dairy and use cool bags with ice packs as a workaround [V/P], Mumsnet/The Student Room.
- **Taste and texture:** the "dry, bland" Trek failure is the exact risk for an oat-based product.
- **Price:** the anchor is £1–2 on offer; fresh food costs more.
- **Eggs:** the smell. Baked-in egg may avoid this `[ASSUMPTION]`.
- **"I already get enough protein":** the British Nutrition Foundation says most UK adults meet protein needs ([The Grocer](https://www.thegrocer.co.uk/analysis-and-features/protein-mania-are-shoppers-getting-too-much-of-a-good-thing/722201.article)).
- **Fitness isn't the main reason most people buy protein:** only 16% name muscle/fitness ([L.E.K.](https://www.lek.com/insights/food-beverage/protein-goes-mainstream-example-where-value-accrues-next-uk-food-and)). So target people who train specifically.

## 5. Hidden motivations ("People say X, but what they need is Y")
- People say **"I want high protein"**, but what they need is **to not feel hungry or run-down between training and the next meal**. The real job is filling the gap (The Student Room, Sherdog).
- People say **"bars are fine, I'm used to them"**, but what they need is **something they don't feel guilty or sick about**: "no better than a chocolate bar", "chemical aftertaste" (Mumsnet).
- People say **"price matters"**, but what they need is **value they can justify**. They'll pay for a bar they like; they resent paying £3 for "a chocolate bar" (UK-Muscle).
- People say **"real food is better"**, but what they need is **real food that's as convenient as a bar**: "real food is better… but sometimes it's really hard to get real food" [V], [Bodybuilding.com](https://forum.bodybuilding.com/showthread.php?t=238780).
- Coaches say **"eat whole food"**, but what squads need is **a whole-food option the coach can hand out** (E1: Grenade boxes went uneaten).

## 7. Five product and offer hypotheses (each one testable)
| # | Hypothesis | Pain it solves | Cheap test |

| H1 | **"No sweeteners, no sugar alcohols, nothing you can't pronounce"** is the lead message (more than "high protein") | 1, 2, 3 | Two Meta ads or two poster versions; compare sign-ups |

## 8. Market context (secondary, check before quoting)
- Protein bars and shakes in UK grocery: **£230m, +7.5%** (Circana via [Asian Trader](https://www.asiantrader.biz/protein-products-uk-sales-2025)).
- **57%** of UK shoppers follow a high-protein diet "to some extent" ([NIQ 2026](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/news-center/2026/uk-shoppers-shift-to-healthier-diets-in-2026-fuelling-growth-in-protein-and-fibre-sales/)).
- **Cottage cheese volume +29.4%**, driven by protein, gut health and UPF scepticism ([AHDB](https://ahdb.org.uk/news/brits-are-crazy-for-cottage-cheese-will-the-trend-last)). This supports demand for real dairy protein (quark).
- **After Joe Wicks' Channel 4 UPF documentary (Oct 2025),** Grenade unit sales fell 15.9% in 2025 ([FoodNavigator, Jan 2026](https://www.foodnavigator.com/Article/2026/01/06/did-joe-wicks-kill-off-demand-for-protein-bars/)).
- **76–78%** worry about UPF (FSA; lowest among 16–34s at 72%) ([Food Manufacture](https://www.foodmanufacture.co.uk/Article/2025/07/09/consumers-concerned-over-food-prices-and-upfs-finds-fsa-survey)).
- **Shake buyers:** post-workout is the top reason at 48% (51% in the North) ([The Grocer 2024](https://www.thegrocer.co.uk/analysis-and-features/workouts-sugar-and-snacking-10-charts-explaining-uk-attitudes-to-protein-shakes/691009.article), Glanbia-sponsored).
