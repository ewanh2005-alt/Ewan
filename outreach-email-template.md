# Outreach Email Template: Recovery Bar Feedback

**Use with:** `outreach-contacts.csv` (Gmail mail merge, or YAMM). Merge fields are in `{{double braces}}` and match the CSV column names.
**Goal:** reach ≥30 survey responses by Week 7, and book 3–5 more interviews (findings report, section F).

---

## Version A: club secretaries / captains / sports officers (main version)

**Subject:** Quick favour for a Durham student project: would your {{sport}} squad try a recovery bar?

Hi {{first_name_or_team}},

I'm Ewan, a student on the Durham Venture School programme. I'm developing a **natural recovery bar for club athletes** and would really value 2 minutes of your squad's time.

**The idea in three lines:**
- **~20 g protein + ~42 g carbs** from whole foods (oats, dates, figs, milk powder, egg white): a proper post-training refuel, with no syrups or protein isolates.
- **~11 g fibre**, more than any other recovery bar with a real carb refuel, from gut-friendly oats, chia and psyllium (no chicory or inulin).
- **A snap bar dosed to your body weight.** It breaks into 4 segments, so a 60 kg athlete and a 100 kg athlete each eat the right amount instead of one-size-fits-all.

I'm also testing a **Squad Box**: a club order that covers a whole mixed-size squad, priced for grassroots and university clubs rather than pro teams.

## Before sending: checklist

- [ ] **Survey link works**, is set to anonymous, and has a privacy line ("answers used only for a Durham student project, deleted after [date]").
- [ ] **Send from your Durham address**, not a personal Gmail. It's more trusted and less likely to land in spam.
- [ ] **Check each contact** in `outreach-contacts.csv` on the club's current site first. Many club officers change every September.
- [ ] **Send in batches of ~20–30 a day**, not hundreds at once. Gmail limits bulk sending, and batches let you fix the wording after the first replies.
- [ ] **Personalise the first line** where you know someone through your referee or coaching network. Those get the best replies.
- [ ] **Claims stay legal** (findings D1–D2). Never say "speeds recovery", "reduces soreness" or "repairs muscle". The template only states nutrition facts.
- [ ] **Log replies** in the CSV `status` column, so nobody gets a second follow-up.
- [ ] **Don't promise free bars** until the food business registration is in place (finding D3: at least 28 days before the pilot).
