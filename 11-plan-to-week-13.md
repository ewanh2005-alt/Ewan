## Team capability: make it visible
- **Changes-of-direction slide:** "What I learned → what I changed"
  - sugar too high → cut it
  - too big → 50 g
  - dry → new method
  - 20 g impossible from fresh food → added dried egg white
  
  That shows you take feedback and adapt.
- **Name your advisers:** the S&C coach, the professor, FADNE (once they reply), and any brand founder who replies (Perkier, Veloforte).
- **Optional:** recruit one helper (a food science or sports science student) for taste tests and baking.

## Impact & Scale: one slide
- **Scale:** start in Durham → North East universities → national weekly box (from `10-scale-plan.md`).
- **Impact:** swap ultra-processed bars for real food. About 54–57% of UK adults' energy comes from ultra-processed food `[S30]`, and every bar sold replaces one.
- **Beyond athletes:** women aged 35–55 (if tested), plus clubs and gyms.

## Risks to manage
- **The product still tastes bad:** this is the biggest risk. Fix it before the taste test. Don't do the taste test before a bake scores ≥7 yourself.
- **No one pays:** ask for small commitments early (waitlist → pre-order).
- **Food safety:** don't sell or give bars to the public until your registration is in and you have Level 2 hygiene. Taste tests with friends are fine; label allergens.
