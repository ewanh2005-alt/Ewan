## 3. THE IDEA (narrowed on my advisor's advice: one specific person, one unmet need)
- **Customer:** MMA athletes (amateur and semi-pro first) in the UK.
- **Moment:** straight **after heavy training**, often between or after double sessions.
- **Product:** a snack eaten after training that provides **high carbohydrate + high protein, low sugar, low saturated fat**.
  - Low sugar and low saturated fat matter because fighters **cut weight** and want to **stay lean even with no fight booked**.
- **Current reality (my observation, verify it):** MMA gyms offer **mainstream protein bars like Grenade**, but fighters want **whole-food options**.
- **Open design question, research both sides:**
  - **(a) "real food only"** (e.g. egg white, oats, puffed rice; no powders, no sweeteners). It's distinctive, but shelf life is short.
  - **(b) "natural, whole protein sources"** (e.g. milk protein, egg white powder) for a longer shelf life, still with no artificial sweeteners or sugar alcohols.
  - Which would MMA athletes value more, and which is more feasible?
- **My current prototype (calculated, not lab-tested), per 50 g:** ~15 g protein, ~22 g carbs, ~3 g sugar, ~1.4 g fat, ~165 kcal. It's twice-baked to keep in a bag all day; allergens are egg and oats.
- **Price being tested:** £2.00–£2.50 per bar.

### B. The need: who needs this and why
5. What does the science say MMA athletes need **after hard training** (carbohydrate and protein amounts and timing)? Cite position stands (ISSN, ACSM/AND/DC, IOC consensus on sports nutrition and on RED-S) and **combat-sport-specific papers**.

9. **Anti-doping and supplement contamination:** UKAD guidance, Informed Sport, and whether fighters worry about supplement contamination. Does that make real food or natural protein more attractive?
10. What do fighters **currently eat after training**, and what do they **dislike** about it (taste, texture, sugar, sweeteners, price, stomach issues, "processed")? Use forums, fighter interviews, nutritionist articles, podcasts and YouTube. **Label anecdote as anecdote.**
