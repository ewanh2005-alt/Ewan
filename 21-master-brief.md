## C. Key ideas explained simply

- **Persona vs early adopters.**
  - The **persona** (Jack) is your typical customer: the most common pattern across your interviews.
  - **Early adopters** are the first people who'll try it. They have the sharpest need and are easiest to reach: coached squads and your MMA club.
  - Early adopters are a slice of the persona.
- **Migraine vs vitamin.**
  - A **migraine** problem hurts often and people actively want it fixed.
  - A **vitamin** is nice to have.
  - For coached athletes, the problem happens after nearly every session, so it's a migraine. For women 40–60 it looked more like a vitamin (`02d`), which is why they're a second segment.
- **The gap in the market.**
  - **What it is:** no UK snack is high in protein, real food, sweetener-free *and* low in sugar.
  - **Why it exists:**
    - Shops need months of shelf life.
    - Real food lasts days, so brands use powders and sweeteners.
    - Real food holds water, so protein per gram is lower.
    - Fresh egg and dairy cost more.
  - **How Graft gets round it:** bake to order and sell direct.
  - **Honest near-misses:**
    - Bio&Me: 9 g soya protein
    - quark pots: not a grab-and-go snack
    - RXBAR: sugary, and US
    - Perfect Bar and Mooski: US only
- **MVP / pilot.** The smallest real test that proves people want it. For you, that's the taste test plus a free squad trial week, not a factory or a shop listing.
- **Mom Test.**
  - Interview questions about what people *actually did*, not whether they like your idea, because people are polite.
  - Only commitments count: a taste-test slot, an intro, their email.
  - Files: `19-mom-test-questions.md` and `20-interview-template-open.md`.
- **Landing page and waitlist.** See D.

## D. The landing page: what it is and how to use it

**What it is:** one web page (`06-landing/index.html`) that explains Graft and sends people to sign up. It's the front door for anyone who wants to know more first: coaches, judges, Instagram, ads.

**The flow**
```
Poster QR / group chat / Instagram
        │
        ├──► Google Form survey (2 min) ──► email left = waitlist ──► invited to taste test
        │
        └──► Landing page (more info) ──► "Take the survey & join" button ──► same Google Form
```

**Why it matters for the judges:** sign-ups are **evidence of demand**: "X people joined the waitlist". The emails are your list of early adopters for the taste test.

**Steps**
1. **Google Form must-haves:**
   - an **email** question (optional)
   - "Are you a coach or club?"
   - the training questions (from `17-research-sprint.md`)
   - a consent line: "We'll only use your email to contact you about Graft. Ask us to delete it any time."
2. **Put the landing page online** (you set this up; it's free):
   - Unzip the landing-page zip.
   - Go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag the folder in.
   - You get a link. Make a free account so it doesn't expire, and rename it (e.g. `graft-durham.netlify.app`).
3. **Share the link:** Instagram bio, group-chat messages, email signatures. The poster keeps pointing straight at the survey: that's quicker for people at the gym.
4. **Measure (weekly):**
   - form responses
   - % who leave an email
   - where they came from (add the question "Where did you hear about this?" with options poster / group chat / Instagram / friend)
5. **Meta ads are optional.** £40 is about 34 clicks, so it's only a hint `[EVIDENCE: WR75]`. Do free channels first.

## F. Risks and honest gaps (say these before a judge does)
- **Taste is unproven.** v1–v4 failed on texture; v6 is untested. This is the biggest risk.
- **No commitments yet** (0 of 10). The poster and squad trial are how you fix this.
- **Numbers are calculated,** not lab-tested.
- **Food safety:** you've decided not to register as a food business, which is your call since you're not selling. For small, occasional taste tests: label allergens (egg, milk, oats; peanut in the chocolate one), keep it chilled, and ask about allergies first. If tastings become regular or you start supplying squads, check with Durham County Council.
- **The name "Graft" isn't trade-mark checked** (BIPC can help). Backup trading name: Graft Kitchen.
