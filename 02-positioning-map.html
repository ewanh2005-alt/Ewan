Reading it honestly: the low-sugar corner is crowded, but every other bar in it is a lab protein bar built on protein blends or isolates, plus polyols (e.g. Grenade 16 g, PhD 20 g, Warrior RAW >10 g per 100 g) or maltitol/sweeteners (Huel, Warrior). Every whole-food or "natural" bar we found sits at 9.5–38 g sugar, including Perfect Bar (US, chilled, 19 g). fettle is the only bar we found in the box made from kitchen ingredients alone. The trade-offs: slightly less protein than the lab bars (~16–17 g vs ~20 g), a fresh bake that needs a fridge, and it costs more than Warrior RAW (~£1). Not plotted (no sugar figure captured): Myprotein THE Re-Fuel (maltitol is its first ingredient), Amacx, HIGH5 and Voom recovery bars (protein blends + glucose/fructose syrups), SiS REGO, chocolate milk. Sources: 02-competitors.csv, checked by web search 7–8 Oct 2026. fettle figures are calculated, not lab-tested.
