# 02 — Ecosystem Interview Guide

**Purpose:** collect the evidence the judges score under *Problem & Market Research*: real behaviour, not opinions about our idea.
**Rules for every conversation (Mom Test):**
- Ask about **the past and specifics** ("last time…"), not the future ("would you…").
- **Don't pitch until the end.** A "yes, I'd buy that" is a compliment, not data.
- Write down **exact words** and **numbers** (prices, how often, what brand).
- Every interview ends with a **commitment ask** (waitlist sign-up, taste test, intro to a coach). A commitment is evidence; a compliment isn't.
- Log every conversation in the template at the end. **Never** paraphrase into a quote.

The five DVS questions are coded on each item: **[P]** problem · **[C]** credibility of solutions · **[B]** barriers · **[M]** market development · **[A]** alternatives.

---

## 1. Potential customers (athletes and gym-goers, 18–34) — target 30 by Week 8

*Opening:* "I'm researching what people eat after training. No right answers. I'm not selling anything today."

1. What did you train most recently? How long, how hard? **[P]**
2. Walk me through the two hours after that session. What did you eat or drink, and when? **[A]**
3. Why that? Who or what told you to do it that way? **[C]**
4. How often is that what you do, out of your last five sessions? **[M]**
5. Has there been a time recovery went badly: sore, flat at the next session, starving later? What happened? **[P]**
6. What do you have in your gym or kit bag right now? **[A]**
7. When did you last buy a protein bar, recovery bar or shake? Which one, where, how much? Did you buy it again? **[A][M]**
8. What's annoyed you about the ones you've tried: taste, price, stomach, ingredients, size? **[B]**
9. Have you ever had stomach trouble from a bar or supplement? Which one? **[B]**
10. Do you read the ingredients? Show me a product you avoid because of them. **[C]**
11. How much do you spend on food around training in a normal week? **[M]**
12. *(Only now show the concept card.)* What's your first reaction? What would stop you buying it? **[B]**
13. If it were on sale at your club or gym for £2.75, would you buy one this week? What about £2.25 or £3.25? **[M]**
14. **Commitment:** "Can I add you to the first-batch list / save you a taste-test slot?"

**Record about each person:** age band, sport/training type, sessions per week, student/working, where they buy food, current post-training habit.

## 2. Coaches and personal trainers — target 5

1. What do your athletes or clients actually eat after sessions? How do you know? **[A]**
2. What do you tell them to eat? What do they actually do? **[P][C]**
3. Which products do you recommend or warn against? Why? **[C]**
4. Does your club or gym ever buy food or supplements in bulk? Who pays, and how much? **[M]**
5. What would make you comfortable recommending a new bar: testing, ingredients, price, taste? **[B]**
6. **Commitment:** run a taste test at your next session? Intro to the club treasurer?

## 3. Sports nutritionists / dietitians (SENr register, university sport staff) — target 3

1. What do you recommend after training for (a) strength sessions and (b) long endurance sessions? Any typical amounts? **[C]**
2. What do you think of bars like Veloforte Forza, Styrkr BAR+ and Grenade for recovery? **[C][A]**
3. How much fibre is too much straight after training? Does the type of fibre matter? **[B]**
4. Would you trust a bar without batch testing (e.g. Informed Sport) for a drug-tested athlete? **[B]**
5. Which claims are you most sceptical of on recovery products? **[C]**
6. What's missing from what's on the market? **[P]**
7. **Commitment:** review our label and recipe? Be named as an adviser?

## 4. Co-manufacturers and ingredient suppliers — target 3

1. Do you make cold-formed (no-bake) bars? With dried egg white and milk powder? **[C]**
2. What's your minimum run, lead time and price per bar at that run? **[M]**
3. What usually goes wrong when a high-protein bar scales up: hardening, shelf life, water activity? **[B]**
4. What shelf life would you expect for this recipe, and what testing would you do? **[B]**
5. Do you handle peanuts or tree nuts? How do you manage allergen cross-contact? **[B]**
6. Can you do a small paid pilot run (e.g. 500–2,000 bars)? **[M]**

## 5. Retailers, gym owners, café and club-shop managers — target 4

1. Which bars sell well here, and which sit on the shelf? Why? **[M][A]**
2. What price points move? What margin do you need? **[M]**
3. How do you choose a new product? Who decides? **[B]**
4. Would you trial a local product on sale-or-return? What would you need: display, minimum order, invoice terms? **[B]**
5. **Commitment:** a 4-week trial slot?

## 6. Investors, mentors, DVS staff — target 2

1. How do you see the sports-bar category? Is it investable? **[M]**
2. What makes a new food brand worth backing at this stage? **[C]**
3. Do you know why RXBAR left the UK, or other UK bar brands that failed? **[B][A]**
4. What evidence would you need to see by Week 13? **[P]**
