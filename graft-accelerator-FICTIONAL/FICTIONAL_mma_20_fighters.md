## What the imagined set *suggests* (hypotheses only, to test with real fighters)
- **H1:** fighters who train double sessions around work lack **time and something portable** (F1, F8, F10, F18). → Ask: "Walk me through your last double-session day."
- **H2:** **stomach comfort** before and after sparring matters, and sugar alcohols get blamed (F3). → Ask: "When did food last make training feel bad?"
- **H3:** **exact, trusted macros** matter for making weight (F2, F14). → Ask: "How do you track food in camp?"
- **H4:** **clean / no-supplement worry** matters to tested fighters (F9). This may be a small segment.
- **H5:** **price** could kill it for students and novices (F4, F19). → Run the Van Westendorp price questions.
- **H6:** **some won't need it at all** (F7, F13). The real share of these is the key unknown.
- **H7:** **adjacent needs** may beat the bar: advice or content (F6, F15), shakes (F12), post-weigh-in food (F16). Your YouTube channel could serve the content need.
- **H8:** **gyms as a channel:** coaches want something to stock with a margin (F11).
