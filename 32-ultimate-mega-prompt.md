# PART 2: THE VENTURE (CURRENT STATE)

**One-line idea (draft):** a real-food, high-protein, high-carb snack for MMA fighters to eat after hard training: low sugar, low saturated fat, no artificial sweeteners or sugar alcohols, and it keeps in a kit bag all day.

**Customer focus:** UK amateur and semi-pro MMA athletes, especially fighters doing **double sessions** (striking, wrestling, BJJ, S&C) and **managing weight** in camp and out of camp.

**Why low sugar and low saturated fat:** fighters cut weight and want to stay lean even with no fight booked.

**Status quo the founder observes (verify):** MMA gyms stock mainstream protein bars such as **Grenade**, but fighters want **whole-food** options.

**Open strategic question (research both):**
- **(a) Real food only:** egg white, oats, puffed brown rice, honey; no powders, no sweeteners. Distinctive, but harder on shelf life and protein density.
- **(b) "Natural, whole protein sources":** e.g. milk protein, dried egg white. Longer shelf life and higher protein density, still with no artificial sweeteners or polyols. Less distinctive, more manufacturable.

**Business model hypotheses:**
- Direct to fighters: gym pick-up, weekly box or subscription.
- **Gym stockists:** the gym fridge or counter, with a wholesale margin.
- **Creator-led distribution** through the YouTube channel.
- Later: combat-sports events, then online.
- **Price under test:** £2.00–£2.50 per bar (calculated ingredient cost of the v13 recipe ≈ **£1.27 per 50 g bar** at retail ingredient prices, before packaging and labour).

# PART 3: PRODUCT AND RECIPE DATA (calculated unless marked MEASURED)

**The physics constraint the founder discovered:**
- Protein per 50 g depends on **how much water is left after baking**.
- At ~40%+ moisture, fresh-only bakes reach ~10 g protein per 50 g.
- 15 g needs either dried egg white or baking down to ~16–30% moisture.
- More carbs at a fixed 15 g protein also needs less water, which makes the bar firmer.

**Customer persona method:** a persona is the typical or most likely customer **based on evidence gathered so far**: a summary of patterns, not an individual. Build it from interview notes by answering:

- **What are they trying to achieve?** Goals; what they want to improve; what success looks like.
