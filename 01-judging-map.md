## 3. How this map sets priorities

1. **Customer evidence is the weakest area and the cheapest to fix.** It touches 1a, 1b, 1c, 4b and 6b. So every later deliverable is built to *generate* evidence: the interview guide (Phase 2), the waitlist form that asks price and flavour (Phase 6), and the pilot metrics (Phase 7).
2. **Competitor proof is already strong.** Phase 2 should tighten and verify it, not expand it endlessly.
3. **Unit economics must be in code** (criterion 3); judges will test the price/margin.
4. **Branding serves criterion 6** but scores nothing directly. Keep it proportionate.
5. **Impact (5a) needs a real idea**, not a line. I'll propose options in Phase 7.
