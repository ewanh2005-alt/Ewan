## 4. THE VENTURE (current state)

**One-liner (draft):** a real-food, high-protein, high-carb snack for MMA fighters to eat between and after hard sessions. Low sugar, low saturated fat, no artificial sweeteners or sugar alcohols, and it keeps in a kit bag all day.

**Customer focus:** UK amateur and semi-pro MMA athletes, especially fighters doing **double sessions** (striking, wrestling, BJJ, S&C) and **managing weight** in and out of camp.

**Open strategic question:** (a) **real food only** (egg white, oats, puffed brown rice, honey) vs (b) **natural whole protein sources** (dried egg white, milk protein; no sweeteners or polyols). The previous analysis recommended **"b-lite": dried egg white added to the v13 base** (dairy-free, faster bake, better shelf life). Re-test this recommendation; don't just repeat it.

**Business model hypotheses:** direct (gym pick-up, weekly box, subscription); gym stockists (wholesale margin); creator-led through YouTube; later events, then online. **Price under test:** £2.00–£2.50 per 50 g bar.

**Recipe data (calculated unless marked MEASURED)**

### 5.3 Previous analysis conclusions (challenge them, don't assume them)
- **Claims (calculated):** v13 per 100 g = protein 30 g, carbs 45 g, sugar **5.8 g**, fat 2.8 g, ~330 kcal. **"High protein" ✓** (36% energy). **"Low sugar" ✗** (limit ≤5 g/100 g); removing the honey probably fixes it (lab must confirm). Low fat ✓ borderline; low sat fat likely ✓; source of fibre borderline; no added sugars ✗ (honey).
- **Market sizing:** top-down £2.6m = current grocery bar spend by combat athletes; bottom-up SAM ~£16m mid = potential. Pitch line: **"£3–16m serviceable market; year-3 target ~£40–80k"**. Don't pitch £92m.
- **Unit economics:** at kitchen scale, direct at £2.50 ≈ 30% gross margin (pre-labour); **gym stockist at a 33% gym margin ≈ negative**. Gym channel only works at wholesale ingredient prices and with a better batch process.
- **Migraine verdict:** unproven. The physiology is credible; felt pain among fighters is untested.
- **Riskiest assumption:** fighters feel this gap enough to change behaviour. **Test:** 8–10 Mom Test fighter interviews (pass: ≥6/10 describe it unprompted; ≥4/10 already spend money or effort on workarounds).

## 8. SOURCE LIBRARY (the "1,000 verified sources" engine)

**Goal:** up to **1,000 credible sources in 20 batches of 50**, each tied to a pitch claim. Quality beats count: a verified 600 beats an unverified 1,000.

**Verification protocol (every source):** (1) it exists: open the URL / resolve the DOI via doi.org or Crossref or PubMed; (2) title, authors, year, venue and volume match; (3) the claim is really there: exact sentence + page; (4) credibility: peer review, official status, method, sample size; (5) currency; (6) independence (funder, conflicts); (7) access route (open, Durham Library, paywalled).

**Search method:** databases first (Google Scholar, PubMed, Web of Science/SPORTDiscus via Durham Library, Crossref) → official (gov.uk, legislation.gov.uk, food.gov.uk, sportengland.org) → industry (Mintel, Statista, Passport via Durham Library) → trade press (The Grocer, Just Food, FoodNavigator, Grocery Gazette). **Snowball** references backwards and "cited by" forwards. **Log every query with its date.** **Saturation:** stop a topic after 3 consecutive searches with nothing new and credible. **Record exclusions** with reasons.

**Grade-mix target:** ≥40% A–B and ≤20% D–E overall. Market-sizing batches will be mostly C; say so rather than force it.
