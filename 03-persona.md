## The 8 interview questions that would most strengthen this persona

1. "Walk me through what you ate and drank in the two hours after your last session." *(current behaviour)*
2. "Out of your last five sessions, how many times did you eat something specific for recovery?" *(frequency = size of pain)*
3. "When did you last buy a protein bar, recovery bar or shake? Which one, where, how much, and did you buy it again?" *(alternatives + price anchor)*
4. "What's annoyed you about the bars or shakes you've tried?" *(taste / gut / price / ingredients)*
5. "How much do you spend on food around training in a normal week?" *(budget)*
6. "Who or what do you trust for advice on what to eat for training?" *(channels)*
7. "Where do you usually buy snacks around training: gym, campus shop, supermarket, online?" *(distribution)*
8. *(After showing the concept)* "Would you buy one this week at £2.75? Can I put you on the first-batch list?" *(commitment, not opinion)*
