**Expert input:**
- **E1 (S&C coach, Team Durham):** athletes are advised to eat whole foods. The ideal bar would have good texture and taste, a high protein:carb ratio, and some fibre for digestion. Students were once given boxes of Grenade Oreo bars and **struggled to eat them** `[EVIDENCE: E1]`.

## Two segments: recommend the **coached athlete** as primary

The evidence supports two distinct segments.

**Recommendation: primary = "Alex", the coached athlete.** Smaller group, but the sharpest need:
- They're *told* to eat whole foods (C7, E1).
- They struggle to cook (C7, C10).
- They want protein and carbs (C1, C10).
- They have a coach who influences what the whole squad eats (E1), which gives a route to bulk "Squad Box" sales.

**Secondary = "Sam", the regular gym-goer.** Bigger and the most positive (5/6), but weaker need, half worried about taste (3/6), and price-sensitive (C5).

**What would change my mind:** if the taste test and waitlist show gym-goers sign up and pay at a much higher rate than athletes, swap them. All three athletes interviewed are male; check whether female athletes describe the same need `[GAP]`.

## What the evidence changes in the product

| Finding | Change made |
|---|---|
| Coach: high protein:carb ratio, some fibre. Professor: ~10 g protein per 100 kcal, high carbs | **Recipe v6:** Ewan's core list (oats, eggs, milk, chia, cocoa, salt, honey or PB) plus extra egg whites, quark, oat bran and pumpkin seeds. Protein density **6.2 g per 100 kcal**; **"high protein"** (25% of energy); ~6 g fibre. The professor's 10 g/100 kcal can't be reached with whole foods *and* high carbs: oats dilute protein. Only isolate-based lab bars reach it, and they're low in carbs. See `04-concept-bar.md` |
| Taste is the top worry (3/10 + coach's Grenade story) | Blind taste test moved earlier: v6 (extra protein foods) vs v5 (core list) vs Grenade, scored on taste and texture |
| Price matters (3/10) | Keep £2.75 but test £2.25–£2.50 in the waitlist and pilot; Squad Box £2.25/bar |
| Size matters to some (C5) | Test a half-bar (45 g, ~125 kcal) option |
| Athletes can't cook; coach influences the squad | Lead pilot with **one coached squad** (via E1 or C7's club) + one gym |

## The 8 questions that would most strengthen this persona
1. *(Athletes)* "What did you eat in the two hours after your last session? And on days you can't cook?" **(biggest gap)**
2. "What exactly does your coach tell you to eat, or avoid, after training?"
3. "When did you last buy a protein or recovery bar? Which one, what price, did you buy it again?"
4. "What's the most you'd pay for a bar you'd eat after every session?"
5. "What makes you stop eating a bar: taste, texture, stomach, getting bored of it?"
6. *(Female athletes)* "Does any of this match your experience?" (no female athletes interviewed yet)
7. *(After tasting)* "Would you swap your usual for this, and how often?"
8. **Commitment:** "Can I put you on the first-batch list / bring a box to your next session?"
