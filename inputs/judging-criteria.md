## Sheet 1 — "Durham Venture School · Judging Criteria"

**Problem & Market Research**
- Is the problem real, significant, clear, and evidence-backed?
- Has meaningful customer discovery/market research been carried out?
- Is there evidence of a real demand or gap in the market?

**Innovation & Value Proposition**
- Is the solution distinctive or differentiated?
- Does it create a clear competitive advantage?

**Feasibility & Business Model**
- Is the business model credible and realistic?

**Team Capability**
- Does the team have the drive, skills, and resilience to deliver?
- Do they demonstrate coachability and adaptability?

**Impact & Scale**
- Does the venture have the potential for positive social, environmental, or industry impact?
- Does the solution demonstrate the potential to scale its impact beyond its initial market or user base?

**Pitch & Communication**
- Was the presentation clear, engaging, and persuasive?
- Did they inspire confidence and excitement?

## Sheet 2 — "Pitching your idea" (Durham University)

- **What is the problem** you are trying to solve?
- **Who experiences this problem?** Specific audience of **Users / Customers**
- **Why will they care?** Why is this a 'Migraine Problem?'
- **How do you know?**
  - **Proof** that you know about the **customers**
  - **Proof** that you know about your **competitors**
- What is your **proposed solution?**
- Who are your **early adopters?**
- **How will you test the market?** What MVP or pilot will you run?
- Who are **you**?
- What is your next **milestone** and **what do you need** to get there?
